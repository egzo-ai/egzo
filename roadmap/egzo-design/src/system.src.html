egzo hub · design system v1 · 2026-10-14

An amber phosphor console for many agents at once.

The hub is a terminal with a little structure around it. Pages are dense, monospaced and calm; colour is reserved for state and for the one thing you can act on. Open terminals live in a left sidebar next to your projects, survive navigation, and take the whole stage when you pick one.

00Principles

  • Terminal firstThe terminal is the product. Hub pages exist to find an agent and get you into it, then get out of the way.
  • Keys belong to the agentInside a terminal, every key reaches the pty. Hub shortcuts go through one leader, Ctrl+], and nothing else.
  • Colour means stateAmber marks what you can act on. Status hues mark state, always next to a glyph and a word.
  • Dense and calm13px base, 32px rows, hairline borders, no shadows except floating layers. Nothing moves unless something changed.
  • Never lose a sessionNavigating, splitting or going full screen never closes or resets a terminal. Only Close does.
  • Say what happenedErrors name the engine, the cause and the next step. Destructive actions list their consequences.

  • stroke2 units for the outlines, 2.5 for the prompt; square caps.
  • corner1.5 on the front container only.

  • wordmarkegzo in Martian Mono Regular, outlined, always lowercase; /hub in Light, muted. x-height = 0.9 × the front container.

Use. Keep clear space of one offset (5 units) on every side. Don't recolour it, add gradients or shadows, round the containers, or put the amber front on a light background (use the light version). 02Colour

Dark is the default: warm CRT black with a P3-amber accent. Light is a printout: warm paper grey, ink text and the same amber keycaps. Swatches show the theme you're viewing; contrast is measured live against --bg. Toggle the theme with the button at the bottom right.

Accent

Status

Rules. Amber fills appear at most once per view region (the primary action, the active tab rule, the cursor). --accent-text is the accent as text: identical to the fill in dark, a dark ochre in light so links pass AA. --brand is the same amber: the logo and the UI share one phosphor. Every text token passes 4.5:1 on every surface in both themes; --border-strong passes 3:1 for control outlines.

Banners belong to a page or region and stay until the cause is gone. Toasts confirm an action you just took: bottom right, 5s, errors stay until dismissed, announced via aria-live. Field errors sit under the field and say how to fix it.

Confirmation for destructive actions

Remove migrate-db?

  • Deletes the container, its network and its workspace.
  • Uncommitted changes in the workspace are lost.
  • Anyone at its terminal is disconnected.

This can't be undone. The audit log keeps a record.

The title is the question with the object's name. The body lists consequences, not warnings. The commit button repeats the verb and the name; focus starts on Cancel, Esc cancels. Role changes use the same dialog and list what the person gains or loses. Forgetting an engine (it deletes every role on its projects) also asks you to type its name.

One left sidebar holds everything you move between: open terminals on top, then projects, then engines and your account. Switching swaps the stage instantly and never unmounts an xterm instance; hidden terminals keep their socket and buffer.

Sizes

Expanded 272px (232px on tablets). Rail 52px: status glyphs with numbers for terminals, two-letter tiles for projects, names in tooltips; toggle with the button in the sidebar head or Ctrl+] b. Full screen hides it. On a phone the sidebar is the menu: a one-line bar with ☰ opens it full screen, and picking anything closes it.

Terminal layouts

Single (default): the terminal fills the stage beside the sidebar. Split: two side by side. Grid: four. The focused pane gets a 1px amber frame and receives keys. Picking a terminal that isn't on screen puts it in the focused pane. Pop out moves the terminal into its own browser window (same socket hand-off; the entry shows "in a window"). Each pane resizes its own pty; the size shows in the pane's footer.

09Keyboard

The constraint: a TUI needs Ctrl+C, Ctrl+W, Ctrl+R, Ctrl+K, Esc, every Alt/Meta combination (readline uses Alt+B, Alt+F, Alt+digits) and Shift+Tab. Browsers reserve Ctrl/Cmd+T, W, N, Tab and digits. So inside a terminal the hub binds exactly one chord.

Inside a terminal: leader

Ctrl+], then one key. It's already egzo's detach escape (and Docker's and telnet's), no shell or TUI uses it, and it's the same key on every OS and keyboard layout because the hub matches event.code === "BracketRight". A HUD lists the keys after a short pause, so beginners see the menu and experts never wait.

Single-key shortcuts never fire while focus is in a field or a terminal. All of them can be turned off in the user menu (WCAG 2.1.4).

Focus order: sidebar (Go to, the terminal list as one tab stop, the project list as one tab stop, engines, account) → page. In a list, ↑/↓ move, Enter opens, Delete closes a terminal. Inside a terminal, Tab goes to the shell; leaving the terminal is the leader, never a trap. Dialogs trap focus and return it to the trigger.

10Iconography

16px grid, 1.5px stroke, square caps, mitred corners, no fills, drawn from straight segments like box-drawing characters. Inline SVG sprite, currentColor. An icon never stands alone without a label or aria-label. Where a Unicode glyph already says it (● ■ ✕ ? ⌁), use the glyph.

11Motion

Motion confirms; it never decorates. Tab switches and terminal focus are instant. Under prefers-reduced-motion every duration becomes 0 and the cursor stops blinking. Hover a card to see the curve.

--dur-instant · 0ms
Tab switch, pane focus, terminal output
--dur-fast · 90ms
Hover and press colour
--dur-base · 150ms
Menus, toasts, leader HUD in
--dur-slow · 240ms
Dialogs (rise 8px, fade)

Loops are status: the "working" glyph pulses at 1.4s, "connecting" blinks with the cursor's steps(1) rhythm, nothing else loops. No CRT effects. No scanlines, flicker or curvature: they cost legibility. The retro feel comes from the amber, the monospace and a faint glow on amber rules in dark mode only (--glow-accent, set to none to turn it off).

12Responsive

≥ 1280Desktop, primary. 272px sidebar with three-line terminal entries, every table column, grid layout of 4 terminals. The sidebar can collapse to a 52px rail.
1025–1279Same layout, content max 1200px with 24px gutters.
721–1024Tablet. Sidebar narrows to 232px (or the rail). Agent status text hides from the table (it's on the agent page). Two-column pages stack. Grid layout is 2×2 at most; terminals stay fully usable with a hardware keyboard.
≤ 720Phone: a one-line bar (☰, page or terminal name, a ? when an agent is waiting, search). ☰ opens the sidebar as a full-screen menu with the same lists, 40px rows. Hub pages work (find an agent, see if it's waiting, stop or remove it); low-priority columns hide, forms go single column. Terminals are out of scope: they open, but aren't designed for touch.

13Accessibility

  • Contrast: all text tokens ≥ 4.5:1 on every surface, both themes; control borders and focus ring ≥ 3:1. Verified live in the colour section.
  • Colour is never alone: every state has a glyph and a word; unreachable also changes the outline to dashed.
  • Focus: 2px --focus ring with 2px offset on everything focusable; panes get an inset ring.
  • Semantics: the sidebar is a nav; the terminal list is a listbox and page sections are a tablist. Terminal entries' accessible names include state ("refund-api, payments on lab, connected, waiting for you").
  • Live regions: toasts are status (errors alert); a question arriving in a background tab is announced once.
  • Terminal: xterm's screen-reader mode is a user setting; the leader is never trapped by it.
  • Targets: 24px minimum; row actions always visible.
  • Motion: honours reduced motion; no flashing above 3Hz (cursor blinks at ~0.9Hz).

14Implementation

Three files, in the project folder under egzo-design/: tokens.css (every value as a custom property, dark on :root, light via prefers-color-scheme and data-theme), components.css (the classes on this page) and icons.svg (the sprite). No framework CSS. Suggested Svelte components map one to one: Button, Kbd, Pill, Badge, EngineBadge, FilterChips, DataTable, Tabs, Field, Banner, Toaster, ConfirmDialog, Menu/CommandMenu, Presence, Timeline, EmptyState, and the workspace pieces Sidebar (TerminalList, ProjectList), TerminalPane, LeaderHUD.

The workspace store lives above the router: { terminals: [{ id: "lab:payments/fix-auth", state, unread, ask }], active, layout, panes, sidebar: "expanded" | "rail" }. Pages render in the stage when no terminal is active; each TerminalPane owns one xterm instance and one WebSocket and is hidden, not destroyed, when not in view. The current busy / taken / error control frames map directly to the pane states above. Persist the open terminal list in sessionStorage so a reload reattaches.
